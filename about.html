The beginning

How this Kitchen started

Siena's Kitchen began as a place to gather the recipes I kept coming back to - the dependable dinners, the very specific cravings and the sweet things worth turning the oven on for.

Over time, that collection became this recipe archive: part practical cookbook, part scrapbook and part invitation to cook something lovely. This section is ready for the real story of how it all began.
