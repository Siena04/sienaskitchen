Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Summary

Siena's Kitchen is a recipe website. It does not ask visitors to create an account or enter payment details. If advertising is enabled, our advertising partner may use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads.

Information and technology we use

Our web host necessarily receives technical information such as IP address, browser type and requested pages in server logs. The site also loads fonts and icons from third-party providers, which may receive your IP address to deliver those files.

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, cookie information, IP address and activity related to ads. This can include measuring whether an ad was shown or clicked and, where you allow it, personalising ads.

Choices and consent

Before advertising is enabled, we will configure the consent message supplied through Google AdSense for visitors in the EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland. It lets eligible visitors accept, reject or manage advertising choices. You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings; doing so may change how ads work.

Where required by law, advertising cookies are not used until consent is collected. You can revisit your choices through the privacy controls provided in the consent message.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information directly. Technical providers and, when active, advertising providers process information to operate the site, serve and measure advertising, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply.
